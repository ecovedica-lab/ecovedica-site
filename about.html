About EcoVedica

Herbal powders and traditional beauty ingredients, made easy to find.

EcoVedica is the brand of ECOVEDICA HERBAL ENTERPRISES, an Indian business offering herbal powders, natural beauty ingredients and traditional herbal products.

Ingredients like amla, shikakai, reetha, multani mitti and chandan have been part of Indian homes for generations. Our aim is to make these ingredients simple to discover and convenient to buy, through marketplaces our customers already use.

How we sell

This website is our product catalogue. Purchases are completed on Amazon, Flipkart or Meesho, where each listing shows the current price, delivery options and return policy. You can also message us directly with questions.
