About EcoVedica

EcoVedica is the brand of ECOVEDICA HERBAL ENTERPRISES, an Indian business offering herbal powders and traditional beauty ingredients.

We bring together ingredients that have been part of Indian homes for generations, from amla and shikakai to multani mitti and chandan, and make them easy to find and buy online.
